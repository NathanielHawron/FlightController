Drone parts

Date: 21/06/2026

Today, I looked for parts to build the drone with. Initially, I wanted to go with coreless DC motors, since it would be really cheap, easy too implement, and I wouldnt have to deal with ESCs or large batteries. However, it seems impossible to find out how much thrust those motors can produce, so picking parts would be guesswork at best, and it would probably take much longer with no garentee it will work.

The next best thing is to go with a 3S BLDC system. I found fairly cheap 3S motors that produce up to 168g of thrust with the tested propellers, which go with the cheapest 4 in 1 ESC I found. For the airplane, I also found a single ESC. Next, I found a 3 inch carbon fiber frame, the props from the motor datasheet, a receiver, and a 3S battery.
